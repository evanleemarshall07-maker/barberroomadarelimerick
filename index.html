About the shop

An ultra-modern room on one of Ireland's prettiest streets.

The Barber Rooms sits right in Adare village, a few minutes from the thatched cottages and Adare Manor. Inside it's bright and contemporary — a proper waiting area with TV, papers and magazines, so you're never rushed in or out.

Vincent and the team handle everything from a weekly tidy-up to a full traditional shave, with the kind of attention that's turned plenty of one-off visitors — golfers, wedding guests, passers-through — into regulars.
